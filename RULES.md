# Project Rules & Design Invariants

1. **NO LABELS & NO BADGES:** Never add badges, pill chips, uppercase kicker labels ("NEW", "FEATURE", "BETA", "POPULAR"), pill tags, or decorative badge containers.
2. **NO 0s LATENCY & NO TECH GIMMICK CLAIMS:** Never use "0ms latency", "0s latency", "quantum speed", "instant zero-lag", "sub-millisecond", or misleading technical hype. Keep copy strictly grounded in physical restaurant operations (table orders, thermal KOT printing, bill settlement, offline reliability).
3. **NO GLOWY / GRADIENT UI:** Keep the UI strictly utilitarian, flat, tactile, and high-density POS style.
4. **NO DEAD / WASTED CARD SPACE:** Never use arbitrary large fixed heights (`min-h-[320px]`) that leave vast empty chasms. Cards must be dense, purposeful, and sized to their content.
